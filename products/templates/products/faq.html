Product Management

Learn how to add, edit, and manage products in your inventory

How do I add a new product?

Go to the Products page and click the "Add Product" button. Fill in the required details and submit. Ensure you navigate via the Categories page, as the category field is auto-filled during navigation.

How do I edit or delete a product?

On the Products page, select the product and use the "Edit" or "Delete" options available next to each item.

How do I view product history or changes?

Click the history icon or link next to a product to view its change history, including actions and the user who made the changes.

How do I manage categories?

Go to the Categories page for your stream. You can add, edit, or delete categories as needed.

How do I generate and use QR codes for products?

Each product automatically gets a QR code. View the product details page to download or print the QR code for inventory management.

System Management

Manage system allocations, bookings, and status tracking

How do I allocate or book a system?

Go to the System Allocation page, select an active system, and fill out the required details. The system will be booked under your name for the selected time period.

How do I release or extend a system allocation?

Use the "Extend" buttons on the System Allocation page to update your booking period.

What do the different product/system statuses mean?

Statuses like Active, Not Active, Issue, Used, Handed Over, and Removed indicate the current state of a product or system. Refer to the legend or status color codes on the relevant pages for details.

How do I use the Opt-Change feature for system allocations?

Admins can use the Opt-Change button on the System Allocation page to reassign a system booking to another participant or user.

How do I view and manage system status history?

Click the info icon next to a system to view its status change history, including timestamps and user actions.

What are NC (Non-Conformance) details and how do I manage them?

NC (Non-Conformance) details track issues or deviations found on a system. You can view NC details for any system and save or update observations, root causes, and corrective actions taken. This helps maintain a quality record for each system.

How do I view and update system metrics?

Each system has associated metrics (uptime, downtime, utilization percentage, etc.) which can be viewed and updated from the system detail page. Admins can update metrics to track performance over time, and these feed into the analytics dashboards.

System Allocation

Manage system allocations, bookings, and participant assignments

What is System Allocation and how does it work?

System Allocation allows you to reserve and book test systems (Z70, Z50, Z90, etc.) for specific time periods. Navigate to the System Allocation page, select the system type, set your start and end dates, and optionally assign a participant. The system will be blocked for your use during the booked period.

How do I view active versus expired allocations?

On the System Allocation page, active allocations (where the end date is in the future) are displayed prominently. Expired allocations are shown in the history section. You can also view allocation counts and trends on the Analytics Dashboard under the "Allocation & Reservations" tab.

What system types are available for allocation?

Available system types include: Z70 Full System, Z50 Table Top, Z90 Full System, Z70/90 Rack System, Z70/90 Table Top, Z90 Table Top, Z30 Table Top, and Z10 Table Top. These can be selected from the dropdown when creating a new allocation.

How do I assign a participant to an allocation?

When creating or editing an allocation, you can optionally select a participant from the "Blocked For Participant" field. Participants are managed separately in the Users section and can be added by admins.

Who can create or modify allocations?

Lab Incharges, Admins, and Super Admins can create, modify, and delete system allocations. Regular users can view allocations but may need elevated permissions to make changes. The Opt-Change feature allows admins to reassign bookings.

Calibration Management

Schedule, track, and manage equipment calibrations with certificates and compliance

What is the Calibration module and why is it important?

The Calibration module helps you track and manage calibration schedules for products, systems, and build servers. It ensures all equipment is calibrated on time, maintains compliance with regulatory requirements, and stores calibration records and certificates. You can set up recurring calibration intervals (days, weeks, months, or years) and get automatic reminders when calibrations are due.

How do I create a calibration schedule?

Navigate to the Calibration Hub or Calibration Schedule page. Click "Add Schedule" and fill in the calibration type (Initial, Periodic, Post-Repair, etc.), associated equipment, interval settings, responsible person, and service provider details. You can also link it to a regulatory requirement for compliance tracking.

What do the calibration statuses mean?

Scheduled: Future calibration planned. Due: Calibration is due within the reminder period. Overdue: Past the due date — needs immediate attention. In Progress: Calibration is currently being performed. Completed: Calibration finished. Deferred: Postponed to a later date. Cancelled: No longer required.

How do I record calibration results and upload certificates?

After performing a calibration, go to the Calibration Records section and create a new record. Enter the result (Pass, Pass After Adjustment, Fail, Limited Use, or Refer for Repair), before/after values, adjustments made, environmental conditions, and cost. You can then upload a calibration certificate with the certificate number, issuing organization, and expiry date.

How do I get notified about upcoming or overdue calibrations?

Each calibration schedule has configurable reminder settings. You can set the number of days before the due date to receive reminders. Notifications are sent to the responsible person and lab incharge. Overdue calibrations can be automatically escalated after a configurable number of days. Check the Calibration Dashboard for an overview of all upcoming and overdue items.

Compliance & Regulatory

Track regulatory requirements, compliance documents, checklists, and alerts

What does the Compliance module cover?

The Compliance module provides end-to-end management of regulatory requirements, compliance documents, regulatory checklists, and compliance alerts. It tracks compliance status (Compliant, Partially Compliant, Non-Compliant, Under Review), stores evidence, and manages audit schedules. You can link calibration schedules directly to regulatory requirements for full traceability.

How do I manage regulatory requirements?

Navigate to the Compliance Dashboard. Add regulatory requirements with details including the regulatory body, regulation name, section reference, description, applicability (products/systems/processes), compliance deadline, and responsible person. Track compliance status and evidence. Overdue deadlines trigger auto-alerts.

How do I upload and manage compliance documents?

Go to the Compliance Documents section to upload policies, procedures/SOPs, standards, audit reports, training materials, checklists, and other documents. Each document has version control, approval workflows (Draft → Pending Review → Approved), effective/expiry dates, and can be linked to specific regulatory requirements.

What are regulatory checklists and how do I use them?

Regulatory checklists are step-by-step verification lists linked to specific requirements. Each checklist contains items with priority levels, evidence requirements, and due dates. Progress is auto-calculated as items are completed. Checklists can be assigned to team members and verified by supervisors. Use them for audit preparation and ongoing compliance verification.

How do compliance alerts work?

Compliance alerts are automatically generated for events like: calibrations due/overdue, certificates expiring/expired, documents needing review, approaching compliance deadlines, and incomplete checklists. Alerts have severity levels (Info, Warning, Critical, Urgent) and can be acknowledged, resolved, or dismissed. View all active alerts on the Compliance Dashboard.

Where can I see compliance analytics and trends?

The Analytics Dashboard includes a dedicated "Calibration & Compliance" tab with comprehensive statistics including calibration status distribution, regulatory compliance breakdown, certificate expiry tracking, and alert summaries. The Compliance Dashboard also provides a consolidated view of all compliance-related metrics.

How does document versioning work?

Compliance documents support full version control. Each time a document is updated, a new version is created that stores the previous content, version number, upload date, and the user who made the change. You can view the complete version history on the Document Versions page, compare versions side by side, and restore previous versions if needed. This ensures a complete audit trail for all document changes.

How do I export a compliance report?

Navigate to the Compliance Hub and use the "Export Report" option to generate a comprehensive compliance report. The report includes all regulatory requirements, their compliance status, associated documents, checklist completion rates, active alerts, and a summary of upcoming deadlines. Reports can be exported for regulatory submissions and internal reviews.

Reservations & Waitlist

Set up recurring reservations, manage waitlists, and resolve booking conflicts

What are Recurring Reservations and how do I set one up?

Recurring Reservations let you automatically book a system on a repeating schedule — Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, or a Custom Pattern. Set the system, time slot, recurrence type, days of the week, start/end dates, and priority. The system will auto-generate individual booking instances based on your pattern. You can also set a maximum number of occurrences or leave it open-ended.

How does the Waitlist work?

When your desired time slot is unavailable, you can join the waitlist. Specify your desired date, start/end time, priority level (Low, Normal, High, Urgent), and reason. You can be flexible on dates or times to increase your chances. When the slot becomes available, you'll be notified based on your queue position and priority. You must respond before the response deadline or the offer moves to the next person.

What happens when there is a booking conflict?

Reservation conflicts (time overlaps, maintenance windows, capacity issues, or priority conflicts) are automatically detected and logged. Conflicts can be viewed on the Allocation & Reservations tab in Analytics. Each conflict shows suggested alternatives and can be resolved, escalated, or dismissed by admins. You can also configure recurring reservations to auto-resolve conflicts by finding the next available slot.

Can I pause or cancel a recurring reservation?

Yes. Recurring reservations support four statuses: Active (generating bookings), Paused (temporarily stopped), Completed (finished all occurrences), and Cancelled (permanently stopped). Individual instances can also be cancelled or skipped independently without affecting the overall pattern.

How do I view reservation and waitlist statistics?

Go to the Analytics Dashboard and select the "Allocation & Reservations" tab. You'll see total allocations, active allocations, recurring reservations, waitlist entries (with waiting/fulfilled/expired counts), conflict statistics, and charts showing recurrence type distribution and waitlist status breakdown.

Account Management

Manage your profile, password, and account settings

How do I change my profile information?

Profile information, including your password and profile picture, can be updated under the 'My Profile' section on the dashboard page.

How do I reset my password?

For security reasons, two-step verification is currently not enabled. To reset your password, go to your Profile page and update it. If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact the admin to request account deletion.

Who can access admin features or manage users?

Only superusers or admins have access to full management features, including user and participant management.

How do I add or remove participants?

Admins can add or remove participants from the Users section using the available options.

How do I manage user roles and permissions?

Admins can view the full user list and promote/demote users between roles (User, Lab Incharge, Admin, Super Admin). Navigate to the Users section to manage role assignments. Each role has different access levels for creating, editing, and deleting content across the platform.

How do I approve or decline new user registrations?

When a new user registers, Admins or Super Admins can approve or decline the registration request from the Users section. Approved users gain access to assigned streams, while declined registrations are removed from the pending list.

How do I grant or revoke stream access for a user?

From the Users section, Admins can grant or revoke access to specific streams per user. This controls which streams and their associated products, systems, and data each user can view and manage.

Data Management

Backup, export, and manage your data effectively

How do I back up my data?

Navigate to the User section and click "Download Backup" to save your data as an Excel file. Backup files are auto-generated whenever a new sheet is uploaded to the application.

How do I download inventory or user data?

Use the download options in the Users section to export data in Excel or PDF format.

How do I upload products in bulk via Excel?

Go to the Users section and use the "Upload Excel" option to import multiple products at once. Ensure your file matches the required format.

How do I restore previous data versions or backups?

Admins can view and restore previous data versions from the Data Version History in the Users section. Click "Restore" next to the desired backup.

How do I view and download system or product history logs?

Click the history icon next to a product or system to view detailed logs. You can also download logs if available.

How do I download inventory with OS and binaries in separate sheets?

Use the "Download Excel" option in the Users section to export inventory data, including OS and binaries, in separate sheets.

How do I export data as PDF?

Use the "Download PDF" option in the Users section to export user or inventory data as a PDF file.

Manage build servers, operating systems, Zenition products, and OS/binary information

What is the Build Servers module?

The Build Servers module lets you manage all build servers across your streams. The dashboard shows server statistics per stream, and you can add, edit, delete, and view detailed information about each build server. The list supports search, filter, sort, and data export.

How do I use the Build & OS Info Hub?

The Build & OS Info Hub is a multi-step wizard: first select a product, then choose a category (OS or Binaries), and finally download or manage the data. How do I manage Operating Systems?

Navigate to the Operating Systems page from the navigation bar. You can add, edit, and delete operating systems scoped to each stream. Each OS entry is linked to specific products and build servers for tracking compatibility.

What are Zenition products and how do I manage them?

How do I configure System Types (MVS, Stand PC, Apps PC)?

System Types can be configured from the admin configuration panel within the Build & OS Info page. Admins can create, edit, or delete system type categories such as MVS, Stand PC, and Apps PC. These types are used to classify build servers and products.

How do I export build server data?

From the Build Servers list page, use the export options to download the build server data. The list also supports DataTables features like search, pagination, and column-based filtering for quick access to specific servers.

Holistic Systems

Cross-stream system tracking, weekly utilization data, and performance insights

What is the Holistic Dashboard?

The Holistic Dashboard provides a cross-stream overview of all systems. It aggregates system data across multiple streams, showing status summaries, utilization comparison charts, and allowing you to view, add, edit, or delete holistic system entries from a single consolidated view.

How do I manage weekly data for holistic systems?

Each holistic system has weekly data entries that include allocation status, utilization percentage, assigned person, hours used, availability hours, and task descriptions. You can update these weekly to track progress, and the data feeds into trend charts and performance analytics.

How do I view system utilization graphs and comparisons?

Click the graph view button on the Holistic Dashboard to open the analytics modal. It offers 4 chart types: weekly utilization distribution, project-based distribution, status pie chart, and cumulative hours line chart. The main dashboard also shows a system utilization comparison bar chart for all systems.

Can I assign projects to specific weeks?

Yes. When updating weekly data for a holistic system, you can assign specific projects or tasks to each week. This creates a project timeline that can be visualized in the project distribution chart, helping you track which projects used which systems and when.

How do I export holistic system data?

The Holistic Dashboard supports exporting data in both Excel and PDF formats. You can also perform bulk updates on multiple systems simultaneously and use the system selector with search and filter to quickly find specific systems.

Booking Hub & My Bookings

Quick booking interface, personal bookings management, and availability checking

What is the Unified Booking Hub?

The Unified Booking Hub is a central interface for managing all your system bookings in one place. It provides quick stats on your bookings, a Quick Book feature for one-click system reservations, and a system availability checker to see which systems are free before booking.

How do I use Quick Book?

Quick Book allows you to reserve a system in just a few clicks. From the Booking Hub, select the system, choose your date and time slot, and confirm. It bypasses the detailed form for straightforward bookings, making it ideal for quick single-day reservations.

How do I check system availability?

From the Booking Hub, use the availability checker to see real-time system availability for your desired date and time. It shows which systems are free, occupied, or in maintenance, along with the current booking holder and remaining time.

What is My Bookings and what can I see there?

My Bookings is your personal booking management page. It shows all your current and past system bookings with booking statistics charts, enables you to view booking details, extend or cancel active bookings, and provides visual analytics on your booking patterns over time.

How do I access the Reservations Hub?

The Reservations Hub is a centralized landing page that links to all reservation-related features: recurring reservations, waitlist management, booking conflicts, the unified booking hub, and reservation analytics. Access it from the main navigation under 'Reservations'.

Personal Trackboard

Kanban-style task management with drag-and-drop, recurring tasks, and personal analytics

What is the Personal Trackboard?

The Personal Trackboard is a Kanban-style task management board for organizing your personal tasks. It features three columns — To Do, In Progress, and Done — with drag-and-drop functionality to move tasks between stages. It includes task statistics, filtering, and multiple view modes.

How do I create and manage tasks?

Click "Add Task" to create a new task with title, description, priority level, due date, tags, and optional recurrence settings. You can edit, delete, or add comments to any task. Tasks can be dragged between columns to update their status, and overdue tasks are highlighted with visual indicators.

What views are available on the Trackboard?

The Trackboard offers multiple views: Board view (Kanban columns), Calendar/Month view (tasks plotted on a calendar), and Compact mode (condensed card layout). You can also toggle the Statistics panel to see charts showing task status distribution, priority breakdown, dependencies, and time distribution.

How do recurring tasks work?

When creating a task, you can mark it as recurring so it automatically regenerates at your chosen interval. This is useful for daily standup notes, weekly reports, or monthly reviews. Completed recurring tasks will create a new instance for the next cycle.

How do I filter and search tasks?

Use the filter controls at the top of the Trackboard to filter tasks by priority (Low, Medium, High, Urgent), due date range, tags, and recurring status. A search bar lets you quickly find tasks by title or description. Filters can be combined for precise task discovery.

Project Status Board

Track project progress with Kanban boards, bulk operations, and team-wide visibility

What is the Project Status Board?

The Project Status Board is a team-wide project tracking tool with a Kanban interface. It allows you to create, edit, delete, and copy projects, organize them into customizable columns representing project stages, and view analytics on project distribution across statuses.

How do I create and manage projects?

Click "Add Project" to create a new project with details like name, description, status, priority, and assigned team members. You can edit or delete any project, duplicate it using the copy feature, and pin important projects for quick access at the top of the board.

What views and modes are available?

The Project Status Board supports Kanban view (projects as cards in columns) and Table view (traditional list with sortable columns). It also includes an analytics panel with project distribution charts, and keyboard shortcuts for power users.

How do bulk operations work?

Select multiple projects using checkboxes, then apply bulk actions like changing status, updating priority, or deleting multiple projects at once. This is efficient when reorganizing many projects after sprint planning or status reviews.

Can I add quick notes to a project?

Yes. Each project card supports quick notes that you can add, edit, and reference at any time. These notes are visible on the project card itself, providing at-a-glance context without opening the full project detail view.

Notes & Collaboration

Create personal notes, share with team members, and collaborate across the platform

How do I create and manage personal notes?

Navigate to the Notes section from the navigation menu. Click "Add Note" to create a new note with a title and content. You can view, edit, or delete your notes from the notes list. Each note has a detail view for reading the full content.

How do I share notes with other users?

Open a note and click the "Share" button. Select one or more users to share the note with. Shared users will be able to view the note in their "Shared with Me" section. You can control who has access at any time.

Where can I see notes shared with me?

Go to the "Shared with Me" page from the Notes section. This shows all notes that other users have shared with you, including the author and sharing date. You can read these notes but cannot edit them unless you have additional permissions.

How do I view notes I've shared with others?

Visit the "Shared by Me" page to see all notes you have shared with other users. You can see who each note is shared with and remove sharing access for specific users if needed.

How do I remove shared access to a note?

From the "Shared by Me" page, find the note and click the remove sharing option next to the user you want to revoke access from. You'll be asked to confirm before the access is removed. The note will no longer appear in that user's "Shared with Me" section.

Floor & Infrastructure

Manage floors, locations, allocation trees, and system tags for physical infrastructure

How do I manage floors?

Navigate to the Floor Management page for your stream. You can add, edit, and delete floors with details like floor name, number, and active/inactive status. The floor list shows all floors as searchable grid cards with quick access to edit or delete actions.

What is the Allocation Tree and how do system tags work?

The Allocation Tree provides a hierarchical view of system organization using tags. You can create, edit, and delete system tags, assign or remove items (systems/products) to tags, and view tag history. Tags can be organized hierarchically to represent physical locations, teams, or logical groupings.

How do I manage stream creation and deletion?

Admins can create new streams from the Manage Streams page on the dashboard. To delete a stream, use the delete option next to it. All stream deletions are logged in the Stream Deletion History page, which records who deleted the stream, when, and provides a detailed audit trail for accountability.

How do I manage locations and assign them to products?

Use the Locations page to add, edit, or delete physical locations. Each location can be associated with specific products and systems. When creating or editing a product, you can select its location from the dropdown, enabling location-based filtering and reporting across the platform.

Track headcount, FTE allocation across projects, months, and years with advanced analytics

What is Resource Allocation and how does the grid work?

Resource Allocation is an FTE (Full-Time Equivalent) tracking grid on the Projects page. It shows each resource person as a row with columns for every month (Jan–Dec). How do I switch between Monthly and Weekly views?

Above the allocation grid, use the Monthly / Weekly toggle buttons. The Monthly view shows 12 columns (Jan–Dec). The Weekly view splits each month into its constituent weeks, showing the FTE distributed evenly across the weeks of each month. Weekly data is derived automatically from monthly values — you only need to enter data in the Monthly view.

How does Quarter Locking work?

Quarter Locking prevents edits to allocation values for a specific quarter. A lock row above the month headers shows Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4 buttons — each spanning 3 months. Clicking a quarter button locks (or unlocks) all three months in that quarter. When locked, all input cells for those months are disabled and highlighted, and the save function skips them. Locking is enforced on both the frontend and backend. Only users with the appropriate Feature Access role can lock/unlock quarters.

How do I add Cell Notes or Comments?

Hold Shift and click any allocation cell to open the Note Editor. You can type a comment explaining why a particular FTE value was set (e.g. "On leave in March" or "Split between two teams"). Cells with notes display a small orange dot indicator in the top-right corner. You can edit or delete notes at any time using the same Shift+Click action. How do I import allocation data from Excel?

Click the Import button in the toolbar to open the Excel Import modal. Select an .xlsx file and the target year, then click Upload. The importer reads each row and matches persons by Employee ID or Name. If a person doesn't exist, they are auto-created with the details from the spreadsheet. Allocation values for Jan–Dec columns are imported for the selected project and year. A summary shows how many rows were imported, created, and any errors encountered.

How do I export allocation data?

Click the Export button in the toolbar. This downloads an Excel (.xlsx) file containing all resource persons and their monthly allocation values for the currently selected project and year. The exported file can be edited externally and re-imported later.

How do I compare allocations across years?

Click the Compare button in the toolbar to open the Compare Years modal. Select two years (Year A and Year B), then click Compare. The modal shows a side-by-side table for each person with their monthly FTE values in both years plus a Delta row highlighting the difference. Positive deltas (increased allocation) and negative deltas (decreased) are color-coded for easy identification.

What is the Demand vs Supply Heatmap?

Click the Heatmap button in the toolbar to open the Demand vs Supply Heatmap. It displays a color-coded matrix with Component Types as rows and Months as columns. Each cell shows the total Employee allocation for that component in that month. Cells are colored green (low utilization), orange (moderate), or red (high utilization/over-allocated). This helps managers quickly spot resource bottlenecks and plan rebalancing.

Can I paste data from Excel using the keyboard?

This is ideal for quickly transferring a block of FTE values from a spreadsheet. Locked cells are automatically skipped during paste.

Approval Workflows

Learn how to submit, review, and manage multi-level approval requests

What is the Approval Workflows feature?

Approval Workflows is a configurable multi-level approval system that can be attached to any entity type — purchase orders, compliance documents, system decommissions, vendor onboarding, inventory changes, asset disposals, calibration waivers, and more. It ensures that high-risk or destructive actions go through proper review before execution.

How do I submit a new approval request?

Navigate to the Approval Dashboard and click the "New Request" button. Select a workflow template, provide a title, description, and priority, then optionally link it to a specific entity (e.g., a product or vendor). Submit the request and it will progress through the template's configured approval steps.

What actions can an approver take on a pending step?

Approvers can take four actions on each pending step:

  • Approve — advances the request to the next step (or completes it if it's the final step)
  • Reject — cancels the entire request
  • Delegate — reassigns the step to another user for review
  • Skip — bypasses the current step (if the step is not mandatory)
What are auto-triggers and how do they work?

Auto-triggers are rules configured by admins that automatically create approval requests when specific events occur. For example, scrapping a product, decommissioning a server, or blacklisting a vendor can be set to require approval. When an auto-trigger fires, the action is blocked until the request is approved. If rejected, the action is discarded.

How do I create a workflow template?

Only admins can create templates. Go to the Approval Dashboard → Settings tab, then click "New Template". Give the template a name and description, then add ordered approval steps. Each step specifies an approver type/role and can be marked as mandatory. You can also set an auto-approve timeout in hours.

What does the Approval Dashboard show?

The dashboard displays stat cards (total, pending, in-review, approved, rejected counts), along with "My Submitted" and "My Pending Action" lists. Admins also see an "All Requests" view. Each request card shows its status, priority, template, and current step progress.

Can I link an approval request to a specific entity?

Yes. When creating a request, you can optionally select an entity type (e.g., Product, Vendor, Build Server) and a specific entity instance. This links the approval to that item, and upon final approval, any enforced action tied to that entity will be automatically executed.

Do I get notified when my request is acted upon?

Yes. Requesters receive notifications whenever an approval step is approved, rejected, delegated, or skipped. You can track the full progress of your request from the Approval Dashboard.
